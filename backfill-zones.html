Zone window backfill

Only zone 6 has structured planting/harvest month windows, so the grid overlays and recommendations work in zone 6 only. Every crop does have prose timing text for zones 3–5 and 7–9; this tool parses that text into month numbers so you can review and correct it before anything is written. Nothing is saved until you press Write.

These are proposals, not facts — review before writing.
Planting months are read from your own prose for that zone.
Harvest months are only filled in when the crop documents a days-to-maturity figure (e.g. “60–85 days from transplant”), computed as planting month + maturity. That covers the annual vegetables and nothing else — trees, berries, herbs and other perennials have no maturity figure, so their harvest is left blank / no data rather than guessed. Fill those in by hand if you know them.
Hardiness is read from wording like “not reliably hardy”, “container only” or “outdoors Jun–Aug only”. Where a crop cannot survive a zone, the grid overlay will show it red for the months it would die.
