Zone window backfill

Only zone 6 has structured planting/harvest month windows, so the grid overlays and recommendations work in zone 6 only. Every crop does have prose timing text for zones 3–5 and 7–9; this tool parses that text into month numbers so you can review and correct it before anything is written. Nothing is saved until you press Write.

These are proposals, not facts. Planting months come from your own prose. Harvest months are derived: the zone 6 harvest window shifted by the same number of months as the planting difference. That is a reasonable rule of thumb, not a horticultural source — please sanity-check rows before writing, especially amber and red ones.
